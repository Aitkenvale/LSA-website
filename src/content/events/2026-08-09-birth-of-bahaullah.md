The Báb was born in Shíráz in 1819, Bahá’u’lláh in Tehran in 1817 — and on the old lunar calendar their birthdays fell on consecutive days. So Bahá’ís mark them together: the herald and the one he foretold.

They’re days for joy, not solemnity. Prayers, music, stories, shared food — and everyone is welcome, whatever you believe. That’s the whole point of a faith built on the oneness of humanity.

In 2026 they fall on Tuesday 10 and Wednesday 11 November, each beginning at sunset the evening before.

The Birth of Bahá’u’lláh will be celebrated from 3:30pm at the Bahá’í Community Centre.
