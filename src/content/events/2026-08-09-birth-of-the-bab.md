Two hundred years ago in a Persian city of poets and merchants, a young man announced that humanity stood at the dawn of a new age — and changed the course of history for millions. On Monday 9 November the Townsville's Bahá'í community warmly invites you to celebrate the Birth of the Báb, who in 1844 declared himself the herald of a new era of unity between peoples and faiths.
